About Bidders Nepal

An independent aggregator of Nepal's public procurement notices.

What this platform does

Bidders Nepal automatically discovers public tender notices published by Nepali government bodies — PPMO, ministries, departments, utilities, provincial portals and local governments — and turns them into one searchable, consistently formatted catalogue with links to the original notice and its public attachments.

The collector runs on a schedule without user interaction. Important national sources are checked every 20 minutes, a full source discovery runs daily, and local-government websites are discovered incrementally. Each run is recorded with per-source results and errors.

What it does not do

  • It does not bypass logins, CAPTCHAs or restricted portals. The official e-GP portal and third-party commercial portals are registered as requiring authorization and are not scraped.
  • It does not invent values. Fields the source does not publish are shown as “Not available”.
  • It does not claim complete coverage. {{ stats.sources.registered }} sources are registered; {{ stats.sources.scanned_successfully }} have been scanned successfully.
  • It is not a government service and does not accept bids. Always bid through the official portal or the agency named in the notice.

Data and review

Collected records are published as pending until an administrator reviews them. A pending record is flagged on every page. Verification status, first-seen and last-updated timestamps, and the full change history are exposed through the public API so clients can judge a record's freshness themselves.

Developers can use the documented JSON API; the mobile app and this website use the same endpoints.
