How Bidders Nepal works

Automation with a paper trail for every record.

01

Discover

Official listings are scanned on a schedule. Inaccessible and login-only portals are recorded, never bypassed.

02

Extract

Title, organization, dates, budget and contact details are parsed. Missing fields stay “Not available”.

03

Attach

Public bid documents, BOQs, drawings and addenda are linked from the notice with format and reachability.

04

Track

Deadline extensions, cancellations and new files are recorded as changes with history and alerts.
