Report an incorrect record

Every tender page links to its original notice. If a record is wrong or outdated, check the source first — the notice itself is authoritative. Data corrections are handled by administrators in the admin review queue.

Include the tender ID shown on the detail page, the field that is wrong, and the official URL that shows the correct value.

Suggest a source

If a government office, municipality or project publishes tenders and is not covered yet, send its official website and the page listing its procurement notices. Only public, official sources are added; login-protected portals are recorded as requiring authorization.
